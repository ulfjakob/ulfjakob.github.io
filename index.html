Ulf Jakob Flø Aarsnes, Ph.D.

Senior Researcher, NORCE Norwegian Research Centre

I work on modeling, estimation, and control for high-consequence energy systems, with emphasis on drilling dynamics, two-phase flow, and hydrogen-integrated maritime energy systems. My approach combines physics-based reduced-order modeling with robust control and optimization methods for real-time decision support.

Current work centers on project coordination of MARINER and model-based methods that bridge theory with industrial deployment.

NORCE profile · ulfjakob@gmail.com · LinkedIn · Google Scholar

Research Interests

Theory and Methods

  • Machine learning, control and estimation, optimization, and data science.
  • Control and estimation of distributed-parameter systems and nonlinear dynamics.
  • Physics-based reduced-order modeling and data-informed model calibration.
  • Delay-robust control and observer design for coupled PDE-ODE systems.

Application Domains

  • Drill-string torsional and axial vibration modeling, monitoring, and mitigation.
  • Control-oriented two-phase flow models for pressure and well-control applications.
  • Optimization and extremum-seeking methods for drilling efficiency.
  • Hydrogen and maritime energy-system integration with model-based operation support.
  • Energy systems and decision support for operational and industrial settings.
